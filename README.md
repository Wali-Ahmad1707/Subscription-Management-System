# Subscription Management System

A web-based platform designed to manage products, subscription plans, customer subscriptions, quotations, invoices, discounts, and payments through a centralized dashboard.

## Project Overview

The Subscription Management System helps businesses manage the complete subscription lifecycle, from creating quotations to activating subscriptions, generating invoices, and tracking payments.

## Key Features

- Role-based access for Admin, Staff, and Customers
- User registration, login, and password reset
- Product and product variant management
- Flexible subscription plans (Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Yearly)
- Quotation creation and management
- Subscription lifecycle tracking
- Automatic invoice generation
- Discount and tax management
- Payment tracking and overdue invoice monitoring
- Dashboard with revenue and subscription reports

## User Roles

| Role | Responsibilities |
|---|---|
| Admin | Manage staff, products, plans, discounts, and reports |
| Staff | Manage assigned customer and subscription operations |
| Customer | View subscriptions, quotations, invoices, and payments |
